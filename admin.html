Platform safety rules

  • Never change a user's role or grant admin access based on user-editable profile metadata.
  • Do not delete job records to make a complaint disappear. Use the review workflow and audit log.
  • Ask for a reason before account suspension or restoration.
  • Do not suspend an administrator through this screen; use a separate owner-reviewed recovery process.
  • Keep access limited to information required for support and moderation. Do not share customer data unnecessarily.
  • Before public launch, add an appeals process, privacy request workflow, MFA enforcement for admins and a second-person review for severe actions.

The audit log is stored server-side. A full audit-log viewer and formal appeal workflow are planned next.
