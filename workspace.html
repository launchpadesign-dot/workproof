How WorkProof works

A Job Passport keeps job details, progress notes, invitations, concerns and decisions in one chronological record. Approved terms should not be silently overwritten.

  • Only the job owner and invited participants can access a job.
  • Invitations are single-use and expire after seven days. Share invite links privately with the intended person.
  • Progress notes and activity events are designed to be append-only. If something is wrong, add a correction note rather than silently rewriting history.
  • Do not upload sensitive identity documents, bank details or other highly sensitive information. Secure photo uploads are not enabled in this beta.
  • For privacy or account deletion requests, contact the WorkProof team through the public website while support processes are being built.

WorkProof is a record-keeping tool, not legal advice, a payment guarantee or a decision-maker in disputes.
