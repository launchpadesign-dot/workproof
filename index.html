Good questions

What you should know

Plain answers before you decide whether WorkProof is for you.

Does WorkProof guarantee that I won't be sued?

No software can guarantee that. WorkProof is intended to help people keep clearer records and reduce avoidable misunderstandings. Legal rights and outcomes depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

Is WorkProof only for contractors?

The first focus is service businesses and independent workers—such as maintenance, cleaning, plumbing, building and repairs—along with the customers who hire them.

Can someone disagree with a record?

A useful record should let people ask questions, request corrections and note disagreements. Acknowledging a record should not be presented as automatically waiving a person's rights.

Is the full product available now?

This is an early-access concept website. We are gathering feedback to decide which workflows to build first. The job shown above is illustrative, not a live customer job.

How will my information be handled?

The production product needs clear privacy terms, appropriate access controls and security testing before handling sensitive job records. Please don't send confidential customer or job details through an unverified form.
